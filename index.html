05 — Why Nextora

Why manage multiple providers when you can work with one connected partner?

Technology is complicated. Working with the right IT partner shouldn't be.

Each supplier holds its own contract, its own SLA and its own escalation path. The gaps between them are yours to manage.

Opening a new office? That means network, telephony, laptops and a support engineer: four vendors, four timelines, one deadline.

One partner. Multiple capabilities. Less complexity.

From cloud and infrastructure to security, disaster recovery, telephony, hardware and workforce solutions, Nextora brings the capabilities together under one roof. Not every business needs every service, but when needs connect, one partner can carry them together.
