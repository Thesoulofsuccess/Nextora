Privacy notice

How we use the details you share with us.

This notice explains what Nextora Technologies Pvt Ltd collects through this website and how it is used.

What we collect

  • When you request our business profile: your name, work email and, if you choose, your company.
  • When you start a conversation: your name, your email or phone number, the topic you pick and any message you write.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends, such as your IP address, kept with your enquiry to help prevent spam.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you the information you asked for.
  • To follow up about Nextora's services that relate to your request.

We do not sell your details. They are shared only with the Nextora team handling your enquiry and with our email and hosting providers.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as they are needed to respond to you and manage our relationship, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the details you have shared at any time by emailing info@nextoratechnologies.com.
